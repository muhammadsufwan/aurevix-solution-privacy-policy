Privacy Policy (PICO)

We at Aurevix Solution respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this Privacy Policy.


Aurevix Solution is an individual developer (Sufwan Saleem) based in Pakistan, and is the developer and publisher of our Games on the PICO Store. In this Privacy Policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to Aurevix Solution, which is responsible for the processing of your information described here.


This Privacy Policy explains what information our apps use, how we process it, where it is stored, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.


This Privacy Policy applies to our virtual reality games and applications made available on the PICO Store ("Games"), and to our website (together "Services"). Our Games on other stores are covered by the privacy policy published for that store.

1Information We Collect

We only use the information our Games need to work. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your email address, phone number, payment details or precise location inside our Games.

PICO Platform Data

Some of our Games use PICO account features. With the permissions you grant, these Games may read your PICO user ID, your PICO nickname (display name) and your profile picture. See section 2 for details.

Game Progress Data

Our Games save your in-game progress, such as levels, experience points, scores, match results, rewards, missions and game settings (for example music and sound on/off).

Leaderboard Data

Some of our Games may have online leaderboards provided by the PICO Platform. In these Games, your score and related game stats (such as experience points and level) are submitted to PICO's leaderboard service together with your PICO user ID.

Device and Motion Data

To play in virtual reality, our Games use headset position, controller position and, where supported, hand tracking data in real time. This data is used only while you play to control the game, and it is not stored or sent anywhere. Our Games do not use the microphone or cameras.

Information You Send Us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

2PICO Platform Data

Games that use PICO account features sign you in with the PICO account already logged in on your headset, through the PICO Platform SDK. No separate sign-up or password is required. Depending on the Game, the data we receive from PICO may include:

DataWhy we use it
User IDTo identify you as the signed-in player and keep your saved profile and game progress linked to your PICO account on your device.
Nickname (display name)To show your name inside the Game, for example on menus, profile screens, in-game scoreboards and leaderboards.
Profile pictureTo show your picture inside the Game, for example on menus, profile screens and leaderboards. The picture is downloaded from PICO's servers and may be saved on your device so it does not need to be downloaded every time.
Leaderboard entriesTo rank you against other players. In Games with online leaderboards, your nickname, profile picture, rank and score are visible to other players of the same Game, and we read other players' entries from PICO to show the leaderboard to you.
Entitlement statusTo confirm the Game was obtained from the PICO Store.

We use this data only to provide these features inside our Games. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not sell it. If this data is not available, or you do not grant permission, the Game uses a default local profile instead.

PICO's own handling of your account data is described in the PICO Privacy Policy, available on the PICO website and in your PICO account settings.

3How We Use Your Information

  • To provide our Games: showing your name and picture, saving your progress, stats and settings, showing leaderboards, and confirming your entitlement. The legal basis for this is the performance of our agreement with you to provide the Game you chose to play.
  • To answer you: if you email us, we use your email address to reply to your question or request. The legal basis for this is our legitimate interest in responding to you.
  • To comply with legal obligations: where required by applicable laws, regulations or legal processes.

4Where Your Data Is Stored

DataStorage location
PICO user ID, nickname, cached profile picture, game progress and settingsLocally on your PICO headset only. We do not run our own servers that store player data, and we do not keep a copy of this data.
Leaderboard entries (in Games with online leaderboards)On PICO's servers, as part of the PICO Platform, handled under the PICO Privacy Policy. PICO states that its services are provided globally and data may be stored outside your country of residence.

5Disclosure Of Information

We do not sell or rent PICO Platform Data or other user information. We do not currently use any third-party service providers, data processors, analytics or advertising services that receive this data.

  • Other players: in Games with online leaderboards, your nickname, profile picture, rank and score are shown to other players of that Game. We do not share your data with anyone else.
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information we hold if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request. Since your data stays on your device or with PICO, we usually hold no such data apart from emails you send us.
  • Changes: if we ever start using a service provider that would receive user data, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so.

6Data Retention

Data saved on your headset stays there until you delete the Game or clear its data. Leaderboard entries stay on PICO's servers until we remove them at your request, or until the leaderboard is reset or removed. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer your request, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

7Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure or destruction. Data is kept on your device or with PICO's services, and is not transmitted to our own servers. However, no security measures can guarantee absolute protection of user information.

8Children's Privacy

Our Games are intended for users aged 13 and older, or the minimum age required to use a PICO account in your country or region if that is higher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9How To Delete Your Data

Every user, in every country or region, has the right to have their data deleted. Deletion is always free of charge. You can delete your data in any of the following ways:

Option 1: Delete the data on your headset

The data our Games store on your device (your PICO user ID, nickname, cached profile picture, game progress and settings) is saved only on your PICO headset. We do not keep a copy of it on any server. You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the Game from your headset, or by clearing the app's data in your headset settings.

Note: uninstalling a Game does not remove your leaderboard entries, because they are stored on PICO's servers. To have them removed, use Option 2 and we will delete your entries from the Game's leaderboards.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

You can also ask us to delete your data by sending an email to sufwan.saleem.meta@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request (PICO)". Please include:

  • The name of the Game you played
  • Your PICO nickname

We will delete any data we hold or control about you, such as your leaderboard entries, emails you have sent us and any related records, and confirm the deletion to you by email within 30 days. If you are unable to delete the data on your headset yourself, we will explain how to do it step by step. We only use the information in your email to process your request.

Option 3: Remove the Game's access to your PICO account

You can stop our Games from accessing your PICO account information at any time by turning off the app's permissions in your PICO headset or account settings.

10Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or other countries with data protection laws), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Delete your personal data (see section 9)
  • Restrict or object to our processing of your personal data
  • Data portability: receive your data in a commonly used format
  • Complain to your local data protection authority if you believe your data is handled unlawfully

To use any of these rights, email us at sufwan.saleem.meta@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days, free of charge. We will only keep data after a deletion request if a law requires us to keep it, and if that happens we will tell you which data was kept and why.
